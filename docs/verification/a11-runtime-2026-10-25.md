This is an observed graphics-call chain, not proof of a specific deadlock or
renderer/COM defect; exceeding the budget does not establish failure to finish
given unlimited time.

## Owner-approved acceptance policy — 2026-10-25

The owner confirmed that the hosted Mac CI has only software rendering, that
complete Mac CAD tests are known to be slow/fail their budgets, and explicitly
agreed to treat this complex-drawing timeout as a **known non-blocking CI
limitation for a11**, rather than requiring its repair/full hidden run before
release. Windows and DockerSW already cover the shared CLI/CAD business chain
and Wine functional acceptance. Do not keep adding equivalent Mac controls or
rerunning this known path merely to seek a pass.

This is an accepted release-policy/risk decision, not a newly demonstrated
technical root cause. Mac-specific integration and lightweight visible-smoke
checks remain relevant; Linux/Wine evidence does not certify macOS graphics,
signing or every hardware configuration. Keep the failed run and 600-second
deadline unchanged, and do not label a selected case or diagnostic as complete
Mac acceptance. No assertion is weakened and no failure is swallowed.
