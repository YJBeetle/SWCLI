wheel/sdist outputs. Upgrade client and daemon together.

## Single-edge constant-radius fillet

`feature fillet ENTITY_ID --radius-mm RADIUS` uses an exact live edge handle,
not a name, coordinate or current selection. It reuses document/session
selection, lease and expected-update-stamp guards and temporary activation.
The first slice is one circular symmetric constant-radius fillet, tangent
propagation disabled, on a writable single-configuration single-solid part.
Existing selections, active edits/commands and incomplete/ambiguous edge
observations are refused before creation. Native round-trip target/settings,
rebuild diagnostics, one solid and finite volume change are checked afterward.

Creation failures may leave partial geometry. Mutation/cleanup evidence and
available exact feature handles remain visible; there is no automatic retry,
delete, save or rollback. Possible topology mutation or uncertain cleanup
retires all old face/edge IDs, even when the host stamp is unchanged. Fresh
observation is necessary before another target choice. Fillet feature handles
do not extend the extrusion-only `feature.inspect/set-depth` semantics.
